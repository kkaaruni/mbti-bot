# mbti-bot
A Discord bot that discovers your MBTI! Find your server's MBTI statistics, compare your MBTI with your friends and see how compatible you guys are!

## Commands

- `!mbti test` starts the MBTI quiz.
- `!mbti stats` shows the server's MBTI statistics.
- `!mbti compare @user` compares the MBTIs of two users and returns their compatibility score.
- `!mbti card` shows a personality card for your MBTI type, or `!mbti card INTJ` to view a specific type.

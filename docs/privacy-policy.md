# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 8, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the MBTI Discord Bot ("the Bot") collects, stores, and uses information when you use the Bot.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you use the Bot's MBTI quiz, the Bot may store the following information:

* **Discord User ID** — used to associate your MBTI result with your Discord account.
* **Discord Server (Guild) ID** — used to associate your result with the server where you completed the quiz.
* **MBTI Result** — the personality type calculated from your quiz answers.

The Bot may also temporarily process your quiz answers while you are completing the questionnaire.

The Bot does not intentionally collect:

* Discord passwords
* Payment information
* Private messages
* Email addresses
* Real names
* Precise location information

## 2. Why We Collect This Information

The information described above is used to provide the Bot's features.

Your Discord User ID and Guild ID are used to:

* Save your MBTI result.
* Retrieve your result when you use commands such as `!mbti card`.
* Compare your result with another server member.
* Include your result in server statistics.

Your MBTI result is used to:

* Display your personality type.
* Assign an MBTI role within a server where supported.
* Calculate server personality statistics.
* Calculate aggregated global statistics.

## 3. How Your Data Is Stored

MBTI results are stored in a database used by the Bot.

The Bot currently uses SQLite for data storage.

Stored records contain information equivalent to:

```text
guild_id
user_id
mbti
```

The developer takes reasonable measures to prevent unauthorised access to the Bot's stored data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 4. Global Statistics

The Bot may combine MBTI results from different Discord servers to calculate aggregated statistics.

For example, the Bot may calculate:

* The most common MBTI type across users.
* The percentage of users with each MBTI type.
* Personality dimension distributions.
* Similarity between server personality profiles.

Global statistics are based on aggregated MBTI results and are intended to describe general trends.

The Bot does not intentionally display your Discord User ID as part of these global statistics.

## 5. Server Statistics

Your MBTI result may contribute to statistics displayed within a Discord server where you completed the quiz.

For example, a server may be shown:

> INTJ: 4 users (20%)

These statistics may therefore reflect the MBTI results of users who have completed the quiz in that server.

## 6. MBTI Roles

If enabled by the Bot and permitted by the Discord server's permissions, the Bot may assign you a Discord role corresponding to your MBTI type.

For example:

```text
INTJ
ENFP
ENTP
```

The Bot may remove a previous MBTI role when assigning a new result.

Role management is performed through the Discord API and is subject to Discord's permissions and functionality.

## 7. Data Retention

Your MBTI result may remain stored until:

* You request that it be removed;
* The developer removes it;
* The Bot's database is reset or deleted; or
* The Bot is discontinued.

The developer may retain limited information where reasonably necessary for technical, security, or administrative purposes.

## 8. Requesting Data Removal

You may request removal of your stored MBTI result.

To request removal, contact the Bot's developer through the contact method provided in the project's GitHub repository.

Please provide enough information to identify the relevant record, such as your Discord User ID and the server in which you completed the quiz.

Once the relevant record has been identified, reasonable efforts will be made to remove it from the Bot's database.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may be hosted using third-party infrastructure providers.

These services may process information as necessary to operate the Bot.

Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own privacy policy and terms.

The developer does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

## 10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information from unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should understand that use of the Bot involves transmitting information through Discord and other infrastructure required to operate the service.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the Bot's features or data practices change.

The "Last Updated" date will be changed when significant updates are made.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

## 12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to request removal of your data, or have concerns about how your information is handled, please contact the Bot's developer through the contact method provided in the project's GitHub repository.

## 13. Acceptance

By using the MBTI Discord Bot, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
